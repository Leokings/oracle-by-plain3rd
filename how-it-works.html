PRODUCT GUIDE

Evidence in.
Decisions out.

Oracle combines a fact-checking contract and a governance contract on StudioNet.

01

Ask with evidence

Add a YES/NO/UNCLEAR rule and public sources. Validators agree on the answer and citations.

02

Review against the charter

Validators check each proposal against the charter version used when it was submitted.

03

Vote only after compliance

The owner opens ballots for compliant proposals. Each wallet gets one vote.

04

Keep every prior ruling

Rechecks preserve earlier decisions. Transaction finality stays separate.
