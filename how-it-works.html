ORACLE BY PLAIN3RD SYSTEM GUIDE

Two decision engines.
One trustworthy workflow.

Oracle by Plain3rd is one product interface over two purpose-built intelligent contracts. It preserves their independent security boundaries while giving users one wallet and one searchable record.

01

Ask with evidence

A question includes an exact YES/NO/UNCLEAR rule and one to five public HTTPS sources. Independent validators fetch those pages and must agree on the outcome and citations.

02

Review against the charter

A proposal is pinned to a specific constitution version. Validators compare untrusted proposal text with that charter and agree on the verdict and cited rule references.

03

Vote only after compliance

The owner may open a ballot only for the latest compliant review. Each wallet gets one vote, quorum is explicit, and anyone may close the ballot after its deadline.

04

Keep every prior ruling

Rechecks never erase old decisions. Oracle by Plain3rd shows application-level review history and GenLayer transaction finality as separate facts, avoiding a misleading appeal label.

Authoritative

Contract state, verdicts, citations, votes and finality.

Explanatory

Leader-generated rationale is clearly labeled non-authoritative.

Protected

Private evidence hosts, embedded credentials and nonstandard ports are rejected.

05

Verify the deployed boundary

Oracle is a single public interface over two independently deployed StudioNet contracts. Keeping their state separate preserves each audited history.
